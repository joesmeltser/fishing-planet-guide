Quick answers

Fishing Planet Guide FAQ.

Everything players need to know before installing the Android companion.

What is Fishing Planet Guide?

A free, unofficial Android companion built around fish hotspot coordinates, complete suggested setups, and an interactive leveling route from level 1 through 105.

Is the app free?

Yes. Fishing Planet Guide is free on Google Play and contains no ads.

How do I install the app?

Open the Fishing Planet Guide listing on Google Play and tap Install. Google Play handles installation and future updates.

Is this an official Fishing Planet app?

No. This is an independent, unofficial community reference created by Smeltser Technologies. Fishing Planet names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
