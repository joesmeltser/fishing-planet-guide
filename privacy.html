Privacy policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Fishing Planet Guide is an unofficial reference application created for the Fishing Planet community. Your privacy matters, and the application is designed to work without requiring a user account.

Information collection

Fishing Planet Guide does not automatically collect, sell, or share personal information. The application does not include advertising or analytics services.

Feedback and email

If you choose to contact us at smeltserspring@gmail.com, your email address and message are handled through your email provider and are used only to respond to your request or improve the guide.

External services

The application or this website may link to external websites, including GitHub and community resources. Those services operate under their own privacy policies, and Smeltser Technologies does not control their practices.

Children's privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no accounts, advertising, or analytics services are included, no age-based profiles are created by the guide.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or privacy practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to smeltserspring@gmail.com.
